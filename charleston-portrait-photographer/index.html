Which kind of portrait session do you need?

These five cover almost every enquiry. They differ in length, direction, and how the images get used, which is what actually changes the plan.

Senior portraits

Two locations, two to three outfits, and a mix of the polished frame parents want and the looser frames you actually want to post. Charleston seniors usually pair a campus or downtown location with something green or coastal so the set is not all one texture. If you are graduating, the graduation photography page covers cap and gown sessions specifically.

Personal branding and headshots

Short, efficient, and built around how the image will be cropped. LinkedIn crops square, most websites crop wide, and press kits want vertical, so a branding session shoots with those three frames in mind rather than producing one photo you then have to cut down. 30-minute sessions start at $40 with 48-hour delivery.

Family and lifestyle

Directed enough that nobody is standing awkwardly, loose enough that children are not being managed for an hour. Hampton Park and the beaches work better than downtown for families, mostly because there is room to move and no traffic to work around.

Couples and engagement

Prompt-driven rather than posed, because couples look like themselves when they are doing something rather than holding still. Cypress Gardens and downtown Charleston are the two directions most couples pick between, and they produce very different galleries.

Creative and studio

Black and white silhouette work, shutter drag, hard flash at night, colour gels. This is the category where the plan matters most, because a concept portrait either works or it does not, and that is decided before the shutter, not in editing.

Popular Charleston portrait locations

Not sure where to shoot? These are the areas I return to, and the reason each one earns it.

  • Waterfront Park and the Pineapple Fountain — open water light, and a landmark that reads as Charleston instantly.
  • White Point Garden and The Battery — live oaks, ironwork, and harbour views within one short walk.
  • Hampton Park — the greenest option and the easiest parking downtown, which matters more with families.
  • Folly Beach and Sullivan's Island — the only locations where you get real backlight and space to move.
  • The historic district streets — pastel façades, iron gates, and cobblestone. Best early, before crowds.
  • Studio — for black and white, silhouette, and concept work where controlling the light is the point.

How a session runs

Four steps, and only one of them happens on the day.

  • Plan. We settle the location, the time, and what the images are for. I send a timing plan and styling notes before the day.
  • Shoot. 45 to 90 minutes, directed the whole way. You will not be left to invent poses.
  • Edit. I cull to the strongest frames and retouch every one that gets delivered, rather than handing over everything and calling it choice.
  • Deliver. A private online gallery with high resolution downloads and print options, inside 7 days.

What to wear in Charleston, specifically

Solid colours and natural fabrics photograph best. Light layers matter here in a way they do not in a drier city, because Charleston humidity is real from roughly April to October and it shows in photographs.

Skip tight patterns and narrow stripes. Charleston backdrops are already busy — ironwork, shutters, cobblestone, Rainbow Row — and a patterned outfit competes with them instead of sitting against them. If you bring a second option, change the tone rather than the whole style, so the two halves of your gallery still look like one session.

Charleston portrait FAQ

How much does a portrait session cost in Charleston?

Sessions start at $80 for an individual session at one location with online gallery delivery. Professional headshots start at $40 for a 30-minute session. Longer sessions, additional locations, more outfit changes, and larger retouched image counts move the price up from there.

How long is a typical portrait session?

Most sessions run 45 to 90 minutes depending on the look and location. A single-location headshot session can be done in 30 minutes. Two locations with outfit changes needs closer to two hours once Charleston walking and parking time is accounted for.

What should I wear for portraits in Charleston?

Solid colours and natural fabrics photograph best, and light layers matter because Charleston humidity is real for most of the year. Avoid tight patterns and small stripes, which fight with busy backdrops like Rainbow Row and ironwork. Bring a second option in a different tone rather than a different style, so both sets look intentional together.

What is the best time of day for portraits in Charleston?

The ninety minutes before sunset is the most reliable window, and the hour after sunrise is the best choice if you want the historic district without crowds. Overcast days are genuinely good for portraits, not a problem, because the cloud layer acts as a softbox over the whole city.

How soon will I receive my gallery?

Portrait galleries are delivered within 7 days. Headshots are delivered within 48 hours. Rush delivery is available on request when you have a print or submission deadline.

Do you help with posing if I am not comfortable on camera?

Yes, and most people are not comfortable on camera, which is normal. Sessions are directed rather than left to you: I give specific prompts and adjustments as we go, so you never have to invent a pose or hold an expression you would not recognise.

What happens if it rains on my session date?

We reschedule at no cost, or move to a covered location if you are working against a deadline. Charleston afternoon storms in summer are usually short, so shifting the start time by an hour often solves it without changing the date.
