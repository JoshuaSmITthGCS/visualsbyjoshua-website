What Can Change the Final Quote?

The biggest pricing factors are coverage time, number of locations, delivery speed, lighting needs, and event complexity. A one-location portrait session in downtown Charleston is simpler than an event that moves between indoor speeches, outdoor portraits, and late-night reception coverage.

Charleston's tourism-heavy calendar also changes the plan. Downtown sessions need timing around foot traffic and parking. Beach sessions on Sullivan's Island, Isle of Palms, or Folly Beach need light and tide considerations. Campus sessions around College of Charleston or The Citadel need a tighter route so you get variety without spending the session walking.

Where to Start

If you know what type of session you need, start with the focused Charleston service page: Charleston portraits for personal sessions, Charleston events for coverage needs, or the broader Charleston photographer hub if you are still comparing options.

DMV sessions in DC, Maryland, and Virginia are still available by request, but Charleston is the primary booking market for current availability.

Charleston Photography Pricing FAQ

Do you require a deposit to book?

Yes. Most sessions need a deposit to hold the date, with the balance due before or on the day of the shoot. I'll confirm the exact amount when I send your quote.

Is travel included in the starting rate?

Travel within Charleston is included. Sessions on the barrier islands, out-of-county locations, or in the DMV area may include a travel fee, which I'll spell out in your quote.

How long does it take to get my photos?

Headshots deliver in 48 hours. Portrait, graduation, and couples sessions typically deliver within one to two weeks. Event coverage timelines depend on volume and are confirmed at booking.

Can I add extra time or locations to my session?

Yes. Extra time, additional locations, or extra outfit changes can be added for an additional fee. Let me know what you want when you book so I can build it into the timeline.

Do you offer package discounts for multiple sessions?

I do, especially for recurring work like brand content, ongoing event coverage, or multiple family members booking around the same date. Ask when you reach out and I'll put a custom rate together.

What forms of payment do you accept?

I accept card payments and standard digital transfers. Payment details are included with your invoice once your session is confirmed.
