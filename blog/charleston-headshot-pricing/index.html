Price individual headshots by use

An individual headshot in Charleston usually starts around $40 to $150. A quick LinkedIn update costs less than a session with multiple outfits, backgrounds, and extra retouching.

Start with where the photo will live. LinkedIn, company bios, speaker pages, dating apps, actor profiles, and brand websites all need different choices.

LinkedIn updates

Use one clean outfit, a simple background, and a crop that works small. Your face should read clearly on a phone.

Website bios

Website headshots need more polish and consistency. If your team appears together, match lighting and crop style.

Build team pricing around efficiency

Team headshots cost less per person when the setup stays in one place and people arrive on schedule. The price rises when the photographer travels, sets up lighting, manages many employees, or offers individual image selection.

A good team day needs a schedule. Give each person a time slot and one clear outfit guide.

Small teams

Small teams work well in 60 to 90 minutes. Keep the background simple and the schedule tight.

Company headshot day

A company day needs a check in process, a waiting area, and a delivery plan for final files.

Know what on site headshots include

On site headshots add convenience. They also add setup time, lighting needs, parking, power access, and room requirements.

Send a phone photo of the room before you book. The photographer needs to see ceiling height, window direction, and available wall space.

Room setup

Choose a quiet room with enough depth between the person and the background. Avoid busy conference rooms if you want a clean result.

Outdoor option

Outdoor headshots work well in shade. Avoid direct noon sun unless the photographer brings lighting control.

Ask what retouching means

Retouching should keep you looking like yourself. Basic edits include color, exposure, crop, and light cleanup. Advanced retouching costs more because it takes more time.

Do not ask for a headshot that looks like a different person. A professional image should feel current when you walk into a meeting.

Basic edits

Basic edits clean up lighting, color, and crop. They should come standard with most headshot sessions.

Advanced edits

Advanced edits handle stray hairs, skin cleanup, glare, background distractions, and more detailed file prep.

Set delivery before the session

Headshots lose value when you wait too long. Ask when previews arrive, when final files arrive, and whether you get web and print versions.

If you need a same week update for a new job, speaker profile, or company launch, say that before booking.

Fast turnaround

Fast turnaround works best when you choose the final image quickly. Delay in selection delays delivery.

File sizes

Ask for a square crop, a web crop, and a full size file when you need flexibility.

FAQ

How much do headshots cost in Charleston?

Individual headshots usually start around $40 to $150. Team and on site headshots cost more because they need more planning and setup.

How much should a LinkedIn headshot cost?

A simple LinkedIn headshot should fit the lower end of the range if you need one outfit, one background, and a fast session.

What should I wear for a professional headshot?

Wear clean, fitted clothing in solid colors. Avoid tiny patterns, wrinkled fabric, and anything that distracts from your face.

Can a photographer come to my office?

Yes. On site headshots work well when the office has a quiet room, enough space, and a simple schedule.

How fast can I get headshots back?

Many individual headshots can be delivered within 48 hours when you request fast delivery before booking.

Do teams need matching backgrounds?

Yes, if the photos appear together on a website. Matching light, background, and crop make the team look organized.
