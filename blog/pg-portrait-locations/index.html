Why PG County Is Full of Great Portrait Locations

The Top 5 Portrait Photography Locations in PG County

1. Lake Artemesia Natural Area

Location: Berwyn Heights/College Park

Best For: Nature portraits, engagement sessions, fitness photos

This 38-acre lake has a 1.35-mile paved trail that gives you multiple distinct backdrops in a single walk. Golden hour light bounces off the water, and the surrounding trees frame your subject naturally in every season.

  • Best Time: Sunrise or 2 hours before sunset
  • Parking: Free at 5200 Berwyn Rd
  • Crowds: Light on weekday mornings

2. Watkins Regional Park

Location: Upper Marlboro

Best For: Family portraits, children's photos, seasonal sessions

At 437 acres, Watkins gives you open fields, wooded areas, historic buildings, and a charming antique carousel all in one park. The Old Maryland Farm section adds rustic character that works well for fall and holiday sessions.

  • Best Time: Early morning or late afternoon
  • Parking: Multiple free lots
  • Special: Works well in all seasons

3. National Harbor Waterfront

Location: Oxon Hill

Best For: Urban portraits, professional headshots, engagement photos

National Harbor gives you a polished waterfront setting with modern architecture, the Capital Wheel, and wide Potomac River views. The plaza areas provide clean, contemporary backgrounds that photograph well for corporate and engagement work.

  • Best Time: Weekday evenings for fewer crowds
  • Parking: Paid garages ($5-20)
  • Tip: Free street parking after 6 PM

4. Riversdale House Museum

Location: Riverdale Park

Best For: Elegant portraits, wedding photos, historic themes

This Federal-style mansion dates back to 1807 and sits on grounds with manicured gardens, brick pathways, and classic architecture. The front lawn and side gardens give you several distinct portrait setups without moving far.

  • Best Time: Spring for blooming gardens
  • Parking: Free on-site
  • Note: Check for event closures

5. Allen Pond Park

Location: Bowie

Best For: Family sessions, maternity photos, casual portraits

Bowie's premier park has a 10-acre pond, walking trails, gardens, and the Bowie Ice Arena. You can move from waterside to wooded areas to open lawn in a single session, which makes it ideal for longer shoots with outfit changes.

  • Best Time: Golden hour for pond reflections
  • Parking: Ample free parking
  • Bonus: Covered pavilions for weather backup

Portrait Photography Tips for PG County Locations

Timing Your Session

The best light for portraits in PG County happens during golden hour, which is the hour after sunrise or before sunset. During summer, aim for sessions before 9 AM or after 6 PM. Midday sun creates harsh shadows and makes everyone squint. I check exact sunrise and sunset times on timeanddate.com before locking in a session slot.

Dealing with Crowds

National Harbor and Lake Artemesia draw crowds on weekends. If you want the location to yourself, go on a weekday morning or during school hours. Most parks are nearly empty before 8 AM on weekdays.

Weather Contingencies

Maryland weather shifts fast. Always have a backup plan ready.

  • Covered pavilions at Watkins and Allen Pond Parks
  • National Harbor's covered walkways and indoor areas
  • Historic house museums for indoor options
  • University buildings (check accessibility first)

Insider Tips from a Local PG County Photographer

  1. Parking shortcut: At Lake Artemesia, park at the Paint Branch Parkway lot for easier access to the best photo spots
  2. Seasonal color: The butterfly garden at Watkins Park blooms June through September with rich, vibrant color
  3. Under the radar: Marietta House Museum in Glenn Dale has beautiful grounds and draws almost no foot traffic
  4. Sunset angle: The west side of Allen Pond gives you unobstructed sunset views
  5. Rainy day option: The Clarice Smith Performing Arts Center at UMD has impressive modern architecture for indoor work
  6. Free props: Watkins Park's Old Maryland Farm has vintage farm equipment that works perfectly for rustic portraits
  7. Early bird reward: Show up at Buddy Attick Lake before 7 AM and you will often see herons and deer along the trail

PG County Portrait Locations FAQ

Do any of these PG County locations require a permit?

No. Personal portrait sessions at parks like Lake Artemesia, Watkins Regional Park, and Allen Pond Park do not require a permit. Historic sites like Riversdale House and Montpelier Mansion may have separate rules for larger productions.

Which location is best if I want to avoid crowds?

Riversdale House Museum and Buddy Attick Lake Park stay quiet even on weekends. National Harbor and Lake Artemesia draw the most foot traffic, especially in the afternoon.

What if it rains on my scheduled day?

I build weather backups into every outdoor session. Covered pavilions at Watkins and Allen Pond, National Harbor's covered walkways, and historic house museums all work as rain contingencies.

Can we visit more than one location in a single session?

Yes, if the locations are close together. Lake Artemesia and Buddy Attick Lake Park, for example, are a short drive apart and pair well in one longer session.

Is parking free at these locations?

Most are free, including Lake Artemesia, Watkins Regional Park, Riversdale House, and Allen Pond Park. National Harbor uses paid garages, though street parking is free after 6 PM.

Do you still book PG County sessions if Charleston is your main market?

Yes, PG County and the wider DMV remain available by request. Charleston is my primary booking focus, but I still travel for DMV portrait sessions.
